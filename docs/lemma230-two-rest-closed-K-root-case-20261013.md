# Lemma 2.30 — exact two-REST English proof with a closed K-root

**13 October 2026. Internal author proof note, not an edit of the frozen 2019 manuscript.**

This refines the native proof in \`docs/lemma230-two-rest-proof-and-core-root-obstruction-20261013.md\`. It gives a complete proof of a **restricted theorem** without assuming the false statement that two transverse Hales–Jewett line images freely cover the coordinate power. The remaining hypothesis is mathematically explicit: **the literal common source REST root must itself be a U-closed member of K**.

## Theorem (two active RESTs and a K-closed literal root)

Let F be a finite exact weak relational source with maximal intrinsic U-size, so USize(F)=|F|<=r+1. Let p:F→X be an ordinary homomorphism-embedding and corrected protected U-homomorphism-embedding into the genuine native Hales–Jewett attachment X=Attach(D,S,R^N,(m_k)_{k∈I}). The two maps are not required globally injective, and the temporary X need not be U-closed.

Assume exactly two distinct copy indices i,j are PRIVATE-active in the ORIGINAL weak source (each witnessed by a tested vertex), and no other attached copy index has an original source vertex in its private exterior. Suppose A∈K is closed and irreducible, its power part projection is protected, the old D is U-closed, K has finite strong amalgamation and is hereditary for U-closed substructures, every K-member is ordinarily irreducible, and D satisfies the previous CLOSED rank-r completion invariant R_r(D).

Let the literal exact REST inverse-image sides and their intersection be

    L = {x∈F : p(x) is not private exterior of copy j},
    M = {x∈F : p(x) is not private exterior of copy i},
    H = L ∩ M.

**Additional assumption (NOT automatic):** the induced exact common source H is intrinsically U-closed AND H∈K.

Then the ORIGINAL F has a corrected (K,U)-completion.

### Proof

**1. Genuine relational free cover of F.** Every actual power-core vertex lies in both RESTs. A private exterior vertex has exactly one native copy index, hence lies in at least one REST. Every native relation tuple is generated by a core tuple (then it lies in both RESTs) or by a single attached old-copy tuple (then it lies in at least one REST). The two induced RESTs therefore form a genuine free relational cover of the entire X. The positive map p pulls it back to an **exact** relational free amalgam

    F = (F|L) *_(F|H) (F|M).

This is CI-green Lean PR #176, and it is fully valid even for CORE tuples crossing the two Hales–Jewett COPY ranges.

**2. Proper and bounded source sides.** The source-private exterior witness of j lies in M but not L; the witness of i lies in L but not M. Thus both sides are genuinely proper induced substructures of F. Since |F|<=r+1, both have at most r SOURCE vertices. This does not claim their OLD projected closed hulls have <=r vertices; one invokes the properly formulated closed-rank completion lemma instead.

**3. TWO ordinary maps of the entire RESTs to old D.** The exact source side F|L excludes private exterior j. Since the original source has only i,j private-active, the only possibly private-active line on that side is i. Choose any parameter coordinate k_i of the genuine line i. Restrict p to F|L, pass to the induced source-active native subattachment on at most that one copy, and evaluate its genuine core at k_i while mapping that old attached copy exterior identically to D. This is an ORDINARY old-Picture homomorphism-embedding f_L:F|L→D, not merely a part map. On F|M choose, independently, any parameter coordinate k_j of line j and obtain f_M:F|M→D. There is no assumption k_i=k_j. The source-active selected fold is CI-green PR #177.

**4. Exact common root and the K condition.** A vertex in H is neither private exterior i nor private exterior j. With no other private-active indices, p sends EVERY H vertex into the actual power core. The protected map on original F restricts to a corrected protected core-root map; the protected power part map completes H in A. This is CI-green PR #178.

But a potentially NONINJECTIVE completion H→A does **not** give the full embedding needed for strong amalgamation. Here the present theorem makes a different, explicit hypothesis: H is already U-closed and a K-member. Since K-members are ordinarily irreducible, the restrictions

    f_L ∘ (H ↪ F|L) : H → D,
    f_M ∘ (H ↪ F|M) : H → D

of the two ORDINARY homomorphism-embeddings become ACTUAL FULL induced embeddings a_L:H↪D and a_M:H↪D: the ordinary homomorphism-embedding definition provides full embedding on every irreducible source test, and the entire H is such a test. Their vertex equations are tautological with the SAME root map q=id_H, not inferred from equality of part labels.

**5. Complete both sides over this SAME root and glue.** Maximal intrinsic U-rank of F passes to all its induced sides. Thus the ordinary side maps f_L,f_M protect closed irreducible tests on each side. The old closed-rank-r invariant, their bounded SOURCE cardinalities, the fact that H∈K is intrinsically U-closed, and the full root embeddings a_L,a_M are exactly the hypotheses of the previously Lean-verified two-ordinary-old-map completion lemma. Strong K-amalgamation glues the resulting side completions along H and yields a corrected K-completion of the ORIGINAL F, with its exact tuples preserved by the real free diagram from Step 1. ∎

## Why this does NOT finish unrestricted Lemma 2.30

The important unproved assertion is not relational freeness of the two RESTs; we have already proved that. It is **K-closedness and compatibility of the entire source REST root**.

The literal H consists of ALL original source vertices projecting to the power core. It can contain arbitrary core words not lying in one old embedded A-letter copy, and can be relationally reducible or fail intrinsic U-closedness. The protected completion H→A from PR #178 may IDENTIFY vertices, whereas strong amalgamation requires full embeddings of a common closed K-object into the two side completions. The CI-green Boolean root regression PR #179 exhibits two core vertices evaluated at two distinct old vertices, forbidding one fixed singleton A-letter factorization. Its small example is not a counterexample to the full Ramsey theorem; larger K-roots might be available.

A concrete next lemma would be: for the exact two-REST root, construct a genuine U-closed K-object Q and full embeddings Q into the two appropriate OLD side closed hulls, with a map q:H→Q satisfying two vertex-level equalities, plus the necessary rank budgets. This is a **strictly stronger boundary theorem** than protected root completability. If the source root is already closed and in K, Q=H works (the theorem above). Without that assumption, the construction is genuinely open. For three or more source-active line indices, even getting old maps on both RESTs needs a further recursive active-history argument.

For the original closed-rank Lemma 2.30, bounding the number of original source vertices in a proper cut is not a bound on the closed side generators of a potentially much larger closed test. Relative root budgets must be proved separately.

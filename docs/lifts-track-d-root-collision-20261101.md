## Two distinct image-closure contracts

The verified `EHNInjectiveSupportMinimizer.lean` uses:

> `hImageClosed`: for *every* **vertex-surjective** native EHN map
> `q : Src → Dst`, `F Src` implies **`F Dst`**.

The printed lift proof assumes instead:

> for **every tuple-surjective** homomorphism-embedding from a forbidden
> structure into `H` (whether or not vertex-surjective), `H`
> **contains a full induced forbidden substructure**.

These hypotheses are **not logically comparable without further
assumptions**. The existing Lean `hImageClosed` has a much stronger
*conclusion* (whole target forbidden), but it only quantifies over
vertex-surjective maps, whereas the printed clause covers all
tuple-surjective maps without requiring vertex-surjectivity.
Tuple-surjectivity is not inferred from vertex surjectivity; conversely
tuple-surjectivity need not imply vertex surjectivity. Moreover,
closure of the vertex range under native function values does not
imply tuple-surjectivity for old relations.

Consequences:

1. `FunctionInputTuplesCoveredByIrreducibleTests` ensures the range
   of a native EHN map is function-closed, and permits factorization
   through the actual induced range. It **does not** ensure that the map
   is tuple-surjective onto that induced range: target relations may
   contain additional tuples not generated by the source.
2. The generated weak image quotient in
   `OriginalPartialWeakImageQuotient.lean` *is* tuple-surjective in
   the original partial semantics and is a weak source HE. The
   inclusion of its full forbidden substructure into the ambient
   target need not be a full embedding or even a weak HE on new
   irreducible tests. This is not cured just by proving the range
   function-closed.
3. Accordingly `exists_injectiveLeftMinimizer_and_strictRightReplacement`
   is a **conditional theorem with stronger hypotheses**, not an
   instantiation of the original closure contract. Its one-step
   right-support drop is also not a final left-minimality contradiction.

## Finite adversarial certificate

`Lifts/RootCollisionFiveCycleRegression.lean` checks finite
combinatorial facts in the explicit two-relation example already
explained in the manuscript repository's
`LIFT-INJECTIVE-IMAGE-AUDIT-20261016.md`:

* source: five-cycle, empty ternary relation;
* target: triangle plus the ternary tuple `(0,1,2)`;
* weak colouring: `(0,1,0,1,2)`, identifying the actual
  two-point minimal-separator root `{0,2}`;
* the map is full on all one-/two-vertex graph tests and misses
  no relation on any irreducible source test;
* no injective map of the five-cycle fits in the triangle, and
  every injective triangle map hits the added ternary tuple,
  preventing a full embedding from the empty-ternary triangle.

The manuscript-side English proof additionally establishes
tuple-surjective image closure for the family `{C3,C5}` by the
shortest-odd-cycle argument. **That universally quantified family
closure and the native relational EHN bridge are not yet Lean
formalized**; the new file must not be advertised as a full
Lean-certified counterexample until those are discharged.

This counterexample refutes the blanket *injectivization inference
in arbitrary targets*; it does not supply a counterexample to a full
free amalgam of compatible maximal canonical lifts, and therefore
does not refute the printed strong-amalgamation conclusion.

`Lifts/EHNPrintedTupleImageClosure.lean` provides
`HasTupleSurjectiveForbiddenImageClosure`, whose conclusion is
`ContainsFullForbiddenSubstructure` rather than `F Dst`.
The family-level contract now exactly omits vertex-surjectivity, as

Theorems
`fullForbiddenSubstructure_of_closedTupleSurjectiveEHNRange` and

compose the full forbidden substructure of an actual closed induced
range into the ambient target. Their **explicit** `hTuple` argument
must certify original-partial tuple-surjectivity onto that *actual*
induced range. This is not derived from ordinary EHN, from raw
vertex surjectivity, or from the source function-input cover.
This file is pending complete CI and axiom audit.

The family-level bridge now uses the **exact original-partial
homomorphism-embedding predicate**. The pinned dependency already
proves `originalPartialHE_iff_EHN` under the native nonempty-value
partial encoding, and the application uses `toOriginalPartial`

domains** would require a richer domain-token representation.

## New independent weak-image route (continuation)

`EHNWeakImageForbiddenLocalTransport.lean` now separates another route
from the earlier **full induced closed range** route.

* `originalPartialHE_weakImage_quotient_data` already gives an
  **original-partial HE** `q : Source → Q` onto the source-generated
  weak-image quotient, **tuple-surjective** (and incidentally
  vertex-surjective). The *printed* `HasTupleSurjectiveForbiddenImageClosure`
  therefore applies directly to `Q` with NO closed-range,
  function-input coverage, or additional tuple-coverage premise.
* The actual closure conclusion is a FULL forbidden copy `P ↪ Q`.
  Its composition with the weak inclusion `Q → Target` is
  injective and positive, but NOT automatically an EHN map.
* The specifically named `ForbiddenFullWeakImagePiecesTransport`
  is the separate hypothesis that ALL full forbidden copies of
  this particular `Q` **do** transport as EHN maps into the
  original target. The conditional theorem constructs an actual
  injective forbidden target witness and proves its original
  A-only **source support never increases**. A global
  support-minimizer corollary uses the same local transport
  condition for all witnesses.

This is a more local possible route for the original maximal
common-lift argument: it asks for EHN reflection only on
**forbidden substructures of the generated quotient**, not a
full embedding of the entire weak quotient and not tuple coverage
of the *full induced range*. That local reflection is still
NOT proved from the printed original hypotheses or from root
maximality. The earlier C5/T target specifically demonstrates
why it can fail for arbitrary targets.

The new lemma is **submitted to CI, not yet kernel-certified**.
Do not claim it proves unrestricted injectivization; it exposes
the remaining local full-test reflection obligation.

## What remains impossible to infer

The theorem does NOT show that all positive-B-rank states have a
one-sided successor; a smaller-root piece may become mixed, and an EHN
map may identify distinct seam roots. Even in the successful flip branch,
the A-only source anchor and eligibility of *the next* state need
a separate proof. The original-left-support minimizer need not
hold at intermediate states, and is not assumed here.

This is not yet an instantiation of #958's universal positive-step gate
and it does not establish unrestricted 2019 Claim 2.

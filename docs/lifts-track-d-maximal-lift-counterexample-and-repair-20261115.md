## 1. Three forbidden structures

The language has two binary relations R and B and one ternary relation T. All displayed binary relations are symmetric and irreflexive; an unordered listed edge denotes both ordered tuples. There are no functions, unary predicates, or nullary symbols.

The family F consists, up to isomorphism, of:

* C: the five-cycle on 0,1,2,3,4 with red edges 01,12,23 and blue edges 34,40, with T empty.
* T_R: the all-red triangle, with B and T empty.
* T_RBB: a triangle with one red and two blue edges, with T empty.

The five-cycle has only singletons and edges as nonempty irreducible full substructures. The triangles are irreducible. All three sources are finite and connected. This finite family is regular.

## 2. The exact printed image-closure hypothesis holds

Let h:F0→H be tuple-surjective EHN, with F0 in F; do NOT assume vertex surjectivity. If F0 is either triangle, irreducibility forces h to be full and injective, giving the required forbidden substructure.

For F0=C, tuple-surjectivity forces T^H empty and every target binary tuple to be generated by a source edge. Vertices outside h(C) are isolated. Fullness on source edges prohibits target loops and prohibits any generated target pair from carrying both colours. The induced structure on h(C) is exactly the generated coloured quotient.

If h is injective, tuple-surjectivity makes C→H a full embedding. If it is not, adjacent source vertices cannot be identified, so one of these five nonadjacent pairs is identified:

| Pair | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| 0=2 | h(0),h(3),h(4) give a T_RBB triangle. |
| 1=3 | h(0),h(1),h(4) give a T_RBB triangle. |
| 0=3 | h(0),h(1),h(2) give a T_R triangle. |
| 1=4 | Impossible: red edge 01 and blue edge 04 acquire the same image. |
| 2=4 | Impossible: red edge 23 and blue edge 43 acquire the same image. |

In the first three cases the displayed vertices are pairwise distinct because each displayed edge is an image of a source edge. Every target pair has exactly the displayed colour, by full source-edge reflection; loops and T are absent. Hence the triangle is FULL, not merely a positive copy.

This proves the printed image-closure clause for every target, including ones with extra isolated vertices. No weak image is falsely identified with a full ambient image.

## 3. Two allowed sides

Let A have vertices a,u,v, all three red edges, no blue edges, and T consisting of all pairwise-distinct triples. Let B have vertices a,b, the blue edge ab, no red edges, and T empty. Their common full substructure X is {a}.

A is forbidden-free: C and T_RBB have blue edges, so cannot even map positively into A; T_R is irreducible and cannot embed fully into A because of the extra ternary relation. B is forbidden-free because every forbidden source has a red edge and B has none.

The singleton is full in both sides: all loops and the triple (a,a,a) are false.

## 4. The common singleton lift is genuinely maximal

Deleting any vertex of C leaves a connected path. Deleting any vertex of either triangle leaves a connected edge. Thus no forbidden member has a one-point separating cut. There are no width-one pieces and no unary added piece symbols.

All added canonical relations have arity at least two and, by the ORIGINAL lift definition, require pairwise distinct root images. Every added relation is consequently false on every tuple from the singleton X, in every ambient canonical lift. In particular, no allowed full extension of A or B can change an added relation on X. Old relations are fixed by full embeddings.

Therefore A and B are actual witnesses for the SAME maximal full singleton lift, exactly as in `lem:amalgamationstr`. Maximality is not an omitted hypothesis in this example.

## 5. Their free amalgam is forbidden

Let D=A+_X B, with vertices a,u,v,b. Define

    f:C→D,  (f(0),f(1),f(2),f(3),f(4))=(a,u,v,a,b).

The red three-edge path maps around the red triangle; the blue two-edge path maps along a–b–a. Every positive relation is preserved. Each irreducible source test has at most two vertices. On every source edge, f is a full injection onto an edge of the same colour: the other colour and all loops are absent, and T is false on tuples with at most two distinct vertices. Singleton and empty tests are full too. Hence f is a genuine EHN map.

Thus D is not forbidden-free despite A and B being witnesses of the same maximal singleton lift. This contradicts the unrestricted original maximal-lift amalgamation lemma.

The root {0,3} is a genuine minimal separating cut of C: the components {1,2} and {4} both have this neighbourhood. The red path piece lies entirely in A, but its two roots both map to a. The original added canonical relation is false at (a,a). This is the collapsed-root failure INSIDE the actual required maximal/common-lift setting.

The finite enumeration finds exactly two forbidden maps into D, the two maps of C obtained by exchanging u,v. Both have original left-exclusive source support 2; neither forbidden triangle maps into D. Thus this obstruction is already a support-minimal forbidden witness, not a nonminimal source that recolouring could simply improve.

## 6. No allowed amalgam, even non-free

Suppose full embeddings i:A→H and j:B→H agree at a. The same map with images i(a),i(u),i(v),i(a),j(b) is EHN: each irreducible source edge lies entirely inside one fully embedded side. Also j(b) cannot equal i(u) or i(v), since a common-root pair would then carry both colours, violating fullness. Therefore no forbidden-free H amalgamates A and B, even allowing extra cross-side tuples or identifications.

Finite maximal lifts on the entire carriers of A and B exist. There are only finitely many piece symbols and relevant tuples; each positive canonical relation persists under full extensions. When maximality fails, select an allowed extension adding a tuple and restrict to the old finite carrier plus its finite realizing piece. Repetition terminates after finitely many new tuples. The resulting maximal lifts still agree on the singleton. An amalgam of them would have a shadow amalgamating A and B, which is impossible.

So the specific original maximal-F-lift class need not even have AP under the printed hypothesis. This does NOT imply that the shadow class has no other precompact Ramsey/amalgamation lift.

## 7. Positive repair: full-range EHN saturation
